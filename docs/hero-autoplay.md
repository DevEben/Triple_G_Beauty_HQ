# Continuous hero story

- Decode hero images, then automatically play Craft, Expression, Detail and smoothly reverse back to Craft on a continuous loop.
- No play, pause or replay buttons, and no automatic document scrolling.
- Scrolling takes over from the current frame and controls the story in either direction. Keep the full sticky scroll journey throughout.
- Scene buttons animate to their chapter in place.
- Suspend the clock offscreen or in a hidden tab. Focusing a story action holds it steady; leaving focus resumes automatic playback unless scrolling has taken over.
- Reduced motion keeps a static opening without autoplay.
