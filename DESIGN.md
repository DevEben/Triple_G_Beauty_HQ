# Triple G design system

Approved reference: Serra Sanctuary on SiteReel. Original adaptation for a braiding studio.

## Color
Espresso #30221F, burgundy #542D2D, terracotta #DCA481, pale stone #F4F1EC, muted stone #E9E2D9, white #FFFFFF. Dark brown overlays ensure white hero text is readable. Burgundy anchors primary booking actions on light surfaces.

## Typography
DM Sans for navigation, body and upright display. Existing Bodoni Moda for selective italic display contrast. Fluid headings up to 96px with tracking no tighter than -0.04em. Body copy stays within 65 characters.

## Composition
Full photographic hero with left-aligned typography, sparse navigation, rounded action pills. Wide gutters and generous vertical spacing. Gallery images remain rectangular with asymmetric proportions. Warm pale sections alternate with espresso and terracotta moments.

## Motion and interaction
Short initial text entrance, subtle image zoom on hover, visible content defaults, reduced-motion alternatives. Native modal menu and image lightbox. User-controlled video, no autoplay audio.

## Responsive behavior
Desktop uses offset image and text columns; mobile stacks copy and imagery with comfortable 20px gutters and 44px minimum controls. Filters wrap. No essential information depends on hover.

## Hero motion correction

The uploaded braiding close-up is the hero image. The original process.webp is restored to the studio section. The opening now uses a sticky full-screen scene, scroll-scrubbed image scale/pan, and three crossfading chapters with chapter navigation and a skip link. Reduced-motion users receive a single static opening with direct collection access. This adapts Serra's pinned scene/chapter structure to a still image; it does not reproduce Serra's custom landscape film.

## Aurel One hero revision

User selected Aurel One as the replacement hero motion reference. The body sections retain the existing brand palette and portfolio. Hero uses three photographic scenes: the supplied process image, a generated finished-look portrait, and a generated macro detail. Upright oversized sans-serif headings travel with scroll; new scenes reveal through moving masks and scale changes. Header contracts into a compact centered navigation bar during the story. Generated campaign imagery does not enter the real portfolio. See docs/hero-image-prompts.md for the asset brief and saved paths.
