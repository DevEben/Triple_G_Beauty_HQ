# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Prospective Triple G clients browsing hairstyles, often on a phone, before requesting an appointment.

## Product Purpose
Show real braiding work and make finding a style and contacting the studio on WhatsApp straightforward.

## Brand Personality
Meticulous, expressive, assured. A premium beauty portfolio grounded in real craft.

## Anti-references
Mara Voss was rejected for palette fit. Avoid generic salon service cards, invented social proof, and a technology-product appearance.

## Design Principles
- Let the actual hairstyle photography establish credibility.
- Use the approved Serra Sanctuary reference for photographic scale, earthy color and calm pacing.
- Make browsing and booking clear at every screen size.
- Preserve Triple G identity and factual business information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable contrast, keyboard access, native dialog focus containment, visible focus, reduced-motion support and responsive layouts.

## Current hero direction
The master branch's full-screen three-chapter opening is the preferred hero. Preserve it when improving the rest of the experience. Use the new supplied photographs and films in the portfolio, owner story and film gallery.
